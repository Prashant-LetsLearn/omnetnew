Frequently asked questions

How do I raise an IT support ticket?

Use the form above for an instant ticket ID, start a live chat, email support@omnetit.in, or call / WhatsApp +91 89206 03270.

Is AnyDesk remote support safe?

Yes. The session starts only after you click Accept, you can watch everything, and you can end it anytime by closing AnyDesk. We never ask for passwords or OTPs.

Do you send engineers onsite?

Yes — onsite engineers are available across Noida, Delhi and Gurugram (Delhi NCR). Remote support is available across India and worldwide.

I'm not an AMC customer. Can I still get help?

Absolutely. We support one-time (casual) requests as well as AMC and managed IT customers. See pricing or raise a ticket and we'll guide you.
