FAQ

Network infrastructure questions we hear often

What network infrastructure services does OMNET provide?

OMNET provides structured LAN cabling, fiber optic networking, enterprise Wi-Fi, network hardware configuration and network security integration for offices, warehouses and commercial environments.

Do you provide LAN cabling and CAT6/CAT6A installation?

Yes. OMNET installs CAT6 and CAT6A structured cabling, network points, patch panels and rack/cabinet setups for office and warehouse LAN infrastructure.

Does OMNET provide fiber optic networking?

Yes. OMNET works with single-mode and multi-mode fiber for backbone, inter-building and server room connectivity, including termination, distribution and testing.

Can you design enterprise Wi-Fi for offices?

Yes. We carry out a wireless site survey and plan access point coverage for indoor, outdoor and multi-floor office environments.

Do you provide wireless site surveys?

Yes. A wireless site survey is a standard first step before designing enterprise Wi-Fi coverage.

Can OMNET connect CCTV systems to the network?

Yes. CCTV surveillance systems can be planned and deployed on the same structured network infrastructure as part of a combined engagement.

Can access control systems use the existing network infrastructure?

Yes, where the deployment supports it. Access control connectivity is scoped as part of the site assessment.

Do you provide network AMC and support?

Yes. OMNET offers network AMC covering preventive maintenance, health checks, switch and router monitoring, Wi-Fi and LAN troubleshooting, and incident support.

Do you provide network installation and configuration?

Yes. Installation and configuration are part of our standard network design and deployment process, scoped to each project.

Which locations does OMNET serve?

OMNET serves businesses in Noida, Delhi and Gurugram on-site, with coordination support available across Delhi NCR and India.
