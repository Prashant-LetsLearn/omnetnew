Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you be our single IT contact for our India office?

Yes. We can act as your primary IT contact in India — handling employee support calls, onsite visits in Delhi NCR, vendor coordination, and reporting back to your overseas IT team. All IT matters for your Indian operations go through us.

What time zones can you support?

Our operations are based in IST (Indian Standard Time, UTC+5:30). We can provide 24/7 remote support, covering UK business hours (GMT/BST), UAE hours (GST), and US business hours (ET/PT). Preferred call windows and escalation hours are agreed in your service proposal.

Do you provide onsite support across all of India?

Onsite engineering is currently focused on Delhi NCR — Noida (including Sector 62 and Sector 63), Greater Noida, Delhi, and Gurugram. Remote support is available 24/7 across India. If your office is outside Delhi NCR, let us know your location and we will discuss options.

How do you coordinate with our overseas IT team?

We work within your existing escalation and communication structure. We can use your ticketing system, join scheduled calls, and report in the format your team prefers. The coordination model is agreed before the engagement starts.

What tools and remote access software do you support?

We work with the tools agreed in your service contract — commonly TeamViewer, AnyDesk, Microsoft Remote Desktop, and Zoho Assist. Cross-border access requirements and tool compatibility are confirmed before the engagement begins.

Do you have offices or engineers outside Delhi NCR?

We are currently based in Delhi NCR. We do not have offices in other Indian cities. Remote support covers all of India; onsite support is limited to the Delhi NCR area. We will not overstate our coverage.

Can you help us set up IT for a new India office?

Yes. We can assist with network setup, internet procurement, device sourcing and configuration, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace setup, and ongoing IT support from day one. Tell us your location, team size, and timeline and we will discuss what is feasible.
