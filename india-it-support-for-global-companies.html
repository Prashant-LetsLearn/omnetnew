Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you be our single IT contact for our India office?

Yes. We can be your main IT contact in India. We handle staff calls, onsite visits in Delhi NCR and vendors, and report back to your overseas IT team.

What time zones can you support?

We work in IST (Indian Standard Time, UTC+5:30). Our 24/7 remote support covers UK (GMT/BST), UAE (GST) and US (ET/PT) business hours. Call times and escalation hours are agreed in your proposal.

Do you provide onsite support across all of India?

Onsite visits cover Delhi NCR: Noida (including Sector 62 and Sector 63), Greater Noida, Delhi and Gurugram. Remote support is 24/7 across India. Outside Delhi NCR? Tell us your location and we will discuss options.

How do you coordinate with our overseas IT team?

We follow your existing process. We can use your ticketing system, join your calls and report in your format. We agree how we work together before we start.

What tools and remote access software do you support?

We use the tools agreed in your contract, usually TeamViewer, AnyDesk, Microsoft Remote Desktop and Zoho Assist. Access rules across borders are confirmed before we begin.

Do you have offices or engineers outside Delhi NCR?

No. We are based in Delhi NCR only. Remote support covers all of India; onsite support covers Delhi NCR. We will not overstate our coverage.

Can you help us set up IT for a new India office?

Yes. We can set up your network and internet, buy and set up devices, set up Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and support you from day one. Tell us your location, team size and timeline.
