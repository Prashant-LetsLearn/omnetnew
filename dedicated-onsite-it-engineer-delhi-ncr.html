About This Service

What a Dedicated Onsite IT Engineer Means

We assign a full-time IT engineer to work at your office on the days and hours agreed. The engineer is employed and managed by OMNET IT SOLUTIONS — you get the hands-on support without the overhead of direct employment, recruitment, or HR management.

Dedicated to Your Office

One engineer assigned to your team, learning your environment, devices, and users — not a shared resource split across multiple clients.

Working Hours You Agree

Days, hours, and start dates are agreed in the proposal. Standard office hours are the norm; other arrangements can be discussed.

Outsourced, Not Recruited

The engineer is employed by OMNET IT SOLUTIONS. You get experienced onsite support without taking on employment responsibilities.

Backed by Our Team

Your engineer has access to our wider technical team for escalation, specialist support, and backup — not working in isolation.

Defined Scope & Reporting

Responsibilities, ticket handling, escalation paths, and reporting are agreed before the engagement starts. No ambiguity about what is covered.

Works With Your IT Teams

The engineer can coordinate with your internal IT department or overseas IT team, following your systems and escalation processes.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the dedicated onsite engineer a permanent employee or outsourced?

The engineer is employed and managed by OMNET IT SOLUTIONS and assigned full-time to your office. This is an outsourced service arrangement, not direct recruitment. HR, payroll, and people management remain with us.

What hours does the dedicated engineer work?

Working days and hours are agreed in the service proposal to match your business needs. Standard arrangements follow regular office hours. Extended-hours or weekend coverage can be discussed as part of the scope.

What happens when the engineer is on leave?

Leave coverage, backup staffing, and replacement arrangements are discussed and agreed in the proposal before the service starts. We will explain exactly how planned and unplanned absence is handled for your specific engagement.

Can the engineer work with our existing IT team?

Yes. Many clients use a dedicated engineer to handle day-to-day end-user support while their internal or remote IT team focuses on infrastructure and projects. Escalation paths and coordination are agreed upfront.

Can you place an engineer for our international company's India office?

Yes. We can place a dedicated engineer at your Delhi NCR office and coordinate with your overseas IT team for escalations, reporting, and tooling. See our India IT Support for Global Companies page for more detail on supporting international businesses.

What IT skills does the engineer have?

The assigned engineer's skills and experience are agreed as part of the proposal. We share a profile for your review. Specialist cloud, server, networking, or cybersecurity work depends on the engineer's background and your service agreement.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing depends on the required skills, working hours, location, and scope. We will discuss terms and share a commercial proposal after understanding your requirements. We do not publish fixed prices because every engagement is different.
