Frequently Asked

Questions we answer on every discovery call

If something isn't covered here, call us or book a free assessment — we'll answer it in the first five minutes.

How much does managed IT cost for a 30 to 100-person office across India?

For a 30-person office, a Fixed-Cost AMC typically lands at ₹35,000–60,000 per month. A 50-person Fully Managed IT engagement (including cybersecurity, cloud admin, and vCIO advisory) runs ₹80,000–1,40,000/month. A 100-person managed IT + managed security engagement is typically ₹1,75,000–3,00,000/month. We issue itemised proposals within 24 hours of the discovery call.

What is the difference between an AMC and Managed IT Services?

An AMC is a defined-scope contract covering day-to-day support and maintenance. Managed IT Services is broader — it includes the AMC plus strategic ownership of cybersecurity, cloud administration, vendor management, licence lifecycle, and IT roadmap. AMC keeps the lights on. Managed IT runs the entire IT function.

Do you sign a written SLA?

Yes. Every engagement ships with a signed SLA covering priority-level response and resolution times, onsite frequency, escalation matrix, exclusions, and reporting cadence. Standard: P1 30-min response / 4-hr resolution · P2 2 hr / 1 business day · P3 4 hr / 2 business days · P4 1 / 5 business days.

What does CERT-In empanelled mean and why does it matter for my business?

CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team, MeitY) maintains a formal panel of cybersecurity service providers vetted on audit skills, response capability, and incident handling. Empanelment is required to conduct security audits for regulated-sector clients and produce valid CERT-In-format incident reports. For your business: faster vendor due diligence, defensible documentation in security audits, and access to CERT-In threat intelligence.

Can you work alongside our existing in-house IT team?

Yes — many mid-market clients engage OMNET as L2/L3 escalation, after-hours cover, project capacity, or specialist depth in cybersecurity, cloud, or networking, while their in-house generalist stays front-line. We scope every engagement around your existing structure so there is no duplication or confusion.

How quickly can you take over our IT?

A standard transition for most SMBs takes 2–4 weeks: discovery and asset inventory (week 1), tooling deployment and documentation (week 2), parallel-run with handover (weeks 3–4), then full ownership. Urgent take-overs can compress to a few days where the environment is well-documented.
