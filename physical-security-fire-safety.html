Why It Matters

Why physical security matters as much as IT security

A locked-down network doesn't help if the server room door is unsecured, or if a fire goes undetected after hours. Physical security is the other half of protecting your business:

Assets need eyes on them

Offices, warehouses and data centers hold equipment, inventory and documents that need continuous, reviewable surveillance — not just a lock on the door.

Access needs to be controlled, not just watched

Knowing who entered a building — and when — matters for security, accountability and audits alike. Shared keys and unmanaged doors make that impossible.

Fire risk is a business continuity risk

An undetected fire can destroy equipment, records and premises in minutes. Early detection buys the time needed to respond safely.

FAQ

Physical security & fire safety questions we hear often

What CCTV solutions does OMNET provide?

OMNET designs, supplies and installs IP and HD CCTV camera systems, NVR/DVR recording, remote video monitoring and storage solutions for offices, warehouses, retail and industrial sites, backed by ongoing AMC.

Can OMNET design CCTV systems for offices and warehouses?

Yes. We carry out a site assessment to plan camera coverage, cabling and storage sized to your office, warehouse or industrial premises.

Do you provide CCTV AMC?

Yes. OMNET offers CCTV AMC covering camera health checks, recording and storage verification, preventive maintenance and fault resolution.

What types of access control systems are available?

OMNET works with biometric, RFID/card-based, fingerprint and face-recognition access control, electronic door locks, visitor management and attendance integration.

Can biometric access control integrate with attendance systems?

Biometric and card-based access control can be connected to attendance tracking where the deployment requires it — this is scoped as part of the site assessment.

What is the difference between conventional and addressable fire alarm systems?

Conventional systems report by zone, while addressable systems identify the specific detector or device that triggered, giving more precise location information. Which is right for a site depends on its size and layout.

Does OMNET provide fire alarm installation and maintenance?

Yes. OMNET supports fire alarm control panels, addressable and conventional fire alarm systems, smoke and heat detectors, and ongoing testing and maintenance, in line with applicable project requirements and local regulations.

Can CCTV, access control and fire detection be integrated?

Yes. These systems can be planned and deployed together as part of a single site engagement, alongside your existing IT and network infrastructure.

Does OMNET provide site surveys?

Yes. A site assessment is the first step for any CCTV, access control or fire detection engagement.

Which locations does OMNET serve?

OMNET serves businesses in Noida, Delhi and Gurugram on-site, with coordination support available across Delhi NCR and India.

Do you provide AMC and ongoing technical support?

Yes. AMC plans cover preventive maintenance, health checks, fault identification, emergency support and periodic reporting across CCTV, access control and fire safety systems.
