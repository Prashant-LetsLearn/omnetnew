About the area

Businesses in Udyog Vihar, Gurugram and their IT

Udyog Vihar is laid out in phases, I to VI, between NH-48 and the Delhi border. Older plots still house garment export houses and light manufacturing, while many buildings have been converted into offices for BPOs, IT services firms and company headquarters.

The result is a lot of busy, densely seated floors — call centres and operations teams where every desk depends on the network — next to workshops and warehouses that need coverage and CCTV.

Common requirements

What businesses in Udyog Vihar usually need

Busy, well-segmented networks

High-density seating needs properly designed switching, VLANs and dependable internet.

Internet failover

Two ISP links behind a firewall so operations keep running when one fails.

CCTV and access control

For production, stores and office entrances, with maintenance under AMC.

Quick fixes for desktops

Preventive maintenance and fast replacement of failed parts to keep seats working.

Secure remote access

VPN and MFA for staff working from home or other sites.
