About the area

Businesses in Noida Sector 62 and their IT

Sector 62 sits on the NH-9 corridor where Noida meets Ghaziabad, next to Noida Electronic City metro station. It is mostly office buildings, IT and ITES companies, institute campuses and training centres. Many small and mid-sized firms rent a floor or two in shared buildings.

So offices here need reliable internet and Wi-Fi in shared buildings, and quick laptop setup for growing teams. They need cloud email and team tools set up properly. And they need security that passes a client's or auditor's checklist.

Common requirements

What businesses in Sector 62 usually need

Wi-Fi that works in shared buildings

In shared towers, neighbouring networks crowd the airwaves. A site survey and well-placed, centrally managed access points fix most slow Wi-Fi.

Two internet links with automatic switch-over

A firewall that uses two internet providers and switches over by itself keeps calls and cloud apps running when one link goes down.

Fast setup for joiners and leavers

New staff need a ready laptop, mailbox and access on day one. Leavers need access removed the same day.

Passing client security questionnaires

More and more contracts ask about MFA (a second check at login), antivirus, backups and updates. We put these in place and document them for you.

Training rooms and labs

Institutes and training centres need lab PCs reset, networks cleaned up and equipment checked between batches.
