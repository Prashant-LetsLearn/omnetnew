IT Support on the Noida Expressway

The Noida–Greater Noida Expressway has become one of NCR's main office corridors, lined with IT parks, SEZs and corporate towers. OMNET supports companies here with managed IT, onsite engineers, secure networks and laptops ready for new joiners.

About the area

Businesses in the Noida Expressway and their IT

The Expressway corridor runs from the Mahamaya Flyover area down to Pari Chowk in Greater Noida, covering sectors such as 125, 126, 127, 132, 135, 142, 143 and 144, plus the newer sectors further south. Many offices here sit inside IT/ITES parks and SEZ buildings with landlord-run infrastructure and strict building access rules, alongside company-owned campuses.

Teams on the Expressway tend to be larger and more cloud-first than average, and many work for overseas clients or as a delivery centre for a global parent. Security, identity and device compliance matter as much as everyday support.

Common requirements

What businesses in Noida Expressway usually need

Identity and device management

Single sign-on and MFA with Microsoft Entra ID or JumpCloud, and device management so company laptops stay compliant.

Working within building and change rules

Visits scheduled and changes documented to fit building access rules and your change-control process.

Seat-ready laptops

Laptops set up, enrolled and handed over ready for new hires; returned devices wiped and recorded.

Resilient connectivity

Firewalls with dual-ISP failover and site-to-site VPN to your other offices.

Working with a global IT team

Local support that follows your parent company's standards, ticketing and escalation process.
