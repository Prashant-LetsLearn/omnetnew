What Makes Gurugram IT Support Different from the Rest of NCR

Enterprise Expectations at SMB Scale

Many Gurugram SMB employees have previously worked at Infosys, Accenture, TCS, or global MNCs based in Cyber City. They expect IT to just work — instant helpdesk response, clean Active Directory, reliable VPN, seamless Microsoft 365. An IT support company that struggles with these basics will face constant friction in Gurugram.

Hybrid and Remote Work Infrastructure

Gurugram businesses have higher rates of hybrid and remote work than Delhi or Noida. This means your IT support must cover cloud identity (Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace), device management (Microsoft Intune, JumpCloud), and secure remote access — not just office IT.

Data Security and Compliance

Many Gurugram businesses handle data for international clients — under GDPR, SOC 2, or ISO 27001 requirements. Your IT vendor needs to understand these compliance frameworks and implement controls accordingly. This is not optional for Gurugram businesses with international client contracts.

5 IT Priorities for Gurugram Businesses in 2026

  1. Zero-trust security model: With hybrid work, perimeter-based security is dead. Implement identity-based access control (Entra ID, JumpCloud) and EDR on all devices.
  2. Cloud-first infrastructure: New Gurugram offices should default to cloud-first — Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, cloud storage, and SaaS applications — not on-premise servers unless there is a specific compliance requirement.
  3. Dual-ISP failover: Single ISP dependency in Gurugram has caused multiple well-publicized outages. A dual-ISP setup with automatic failover (using SD-WAN or a proper firewall) costs under Rs 5,000/month and eliminates connectivity as a single point of failure.
  4. MDM for device management: With hybrid work, every employee device needs Mobile Device Management (Microsoft Intune or JumpCloud) to enforce encryption, remote wipe, and patch compliance.
  5. Monthly IT MIS reports: Gurugram leadership teams expect data. Demand a monthly IT report covering uptime, ticket volumes, SLA performance, and open risks.
