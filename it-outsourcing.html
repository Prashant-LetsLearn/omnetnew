FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IT outsourcing?

IT outsourcing means handing some or all of your IT function to a specialist provider instead of running it entirely in-house. With OMNET that can range from a co-managed model (we support your in-house team) to fully managed IT, where we own the helpdesk, security, cloud, and IT strategy under a written SLA.

How is IT outsourcing different from AMC?

An AMC is a defined-scope maintenance contract for a known environment. IT outsourcing is broader — it includes the AMC plus strategic ownership of cybersecurity, cloud administration, vendor and license management, and the IT roadmap. AMC keeps the lights on; outsourcing runs the IT function.

Will outsourcing IT mean losing control?

No. You keep full ownership and visibility. We provide transparent monthly reporting, a written SLA, and quarterly business reviews. You decide strategy; we execute and advise. Many clients co-manage — keeping an internal lead while we provide depth and after-hours cover.

Is my data safe when I outsource IT?

Yes. As a CERT-In empanelled provider we apply least-privilege access, documented change control, endpoint and email security, and incident-response procedures. Data-handling terms and confidentiality are written into every contract.

Can you outsource IT for a company outside Delhi NCR?

Yes. We deliver remote IT outsourcing pan-India and globally, with onsite coverage in Delhi NCR and major metros through our engineer network. A single SLA can cover all your locations.
