IT AMC vs In-House IT Team: What's Better for Noida Businesses in 2026?

August 2026 9 min read Noida, Delhi NCR

If you run a business in Noida — whether in Sector 62, Sector 63, Sector 18, or the NSEZ — you have probably asked: should I hire a full-time IT person, or sign an IT AMC with an IT company?

It is a fair question. Both options have pros and cons. The wrong choice can cost you a lot every year — in salary you did not need, or in IT problems that slow your whole team.

At OMNET IT Solutions, we have had this talk with hundreds of businesses across Noida, Greater Noida and Delhi NCR. Here is the honest answer.

Quick answer: For most Noida businesses with under 60 employees, IT AMC costs less, covers more and is more reliable. In some cases a mix of both (a hybrid model) works better. Read on for details.

What Exactly Is IT AMC?

An IT Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is a fixed-fee contract with an IT company. You pay monthly or yearly, and they take care of:

  • Day-to-day IT help for your staff (remote and onsite)
  • Regular check-up visits to your Noida office, to stop problems early
  • Finding and fixing hardware faults
  • Software updates, Windows/OS security patches and antivirus
  • Watching your network and basic cybersecurity
  • An SLA (our written promise on response time), e.g. 20-minute remote, 4-hour onsite

A good AMC lists every covered device by name and serial number. It states clearly what is included and what costs extra. And it comes with a written SLA.

The Full Cost of Hiring In-House IT in Noida (2026)

Salary is only one part of the cost of a junior-to-mid level IT support hire in Noida. Every month you also pay for:

  • Gross salary (IT Support Engineer, Noida)
  • Employer PF contribution (12% of basic)
  • ESIC (if applicable)
  • Gratuity provision (4.81%)
  • Medical/group insurance
  • Recruitment cost (spread over time)
  • Training and certifications

And that is one person. They can only be in one place at a time. They are off on weekends, sick days and public holidays. In Noida, IT support staff stay 14-18 months on average. When they quit, you start hiring again.

With an AMC, you get a full team of engineers, from first-line to senior (L1, L2 and L3), available 24/7. You also get onsite visits, a ticket system, monthly reports, and monitoring that catches problems before they hit you.

5 Reasons Noida SMBs Choose AMC Over In-House IT

1. You Get More Expertise for Less Money

One IT hire knows a bit of everything. An AMC gives you specialists in networking, cybersecurity, cloud (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Azure), servers and hardware. When your Cisco switch fails at 11 PM, or your Microsoft 365 account is hacked, you need the right expert.

2. No Holiday Coverage Gaps

Your in-house IT engineer takes leave, falls sick, or quits in your busiest month. With an AMC, cover is written into the contract: 24/7, 365 days, including Diwali, Holi and Noida bandhs. The SLA does not take leave.

3. No HR Headaches

Hiring and keeping IT staff in Noida is hard. Engineers often leave for better pay. With an AMC, staff turnover is your vendor's problem, not yours.

4. Predictable Monthly IT Spend

An AMC gives you a fixed IT cost every month. It is easy to budget and easy to explain to your board. No surprise bills when a server fails or an engineer quits.

5. It Grows With You

Opening a second office in Greater Noida? Adding 20 laptops? Moving to remote work? Your AMC vendor adjusts. One in-house hire cannot do more without another hire and another salary.

When Does Hiring In-House IT Make Sense?

  • 100+ employee businesses with enough IT work to keep full-time IT staff busy
  • Highly regulated industries where rules require a cleared employee onsite
  • Businesses with very specialised in-house systems where deep knowledge of those systems is essential

Even then, most businesses use a hybrid model: one in-house IT manager to coordinate, backed by an AMC for everyday helpdesk work (L1) and expert cases (L3).

What to Look for in a Noida IT AMC Contract

  1. Device-level scope - Every covered device listed by name and serial number
  2. Written SLA - Clear response and fix times for each priority level
  3. Exclusions clearly listed - Parts, third-party licences and extra work spelled out
  4. Monthly reporting - Ticket volume, SLA performance, open issues
  5. Named account manager - One person responsible for your account
  6. Exit clause - Fair notice period and full handover of your IT records
