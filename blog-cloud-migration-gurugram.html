Cloud Migration for Gurugram Businesses: Microsoft 365, Azure & Google Workspace (2026 Guide)

August 2026 8 min read Gurugram, Haryana, India

Since 2022, many more Gurugram businesses have moved to the cloud. Reasons include hybrid work, unreliable internet links and cheaper Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace licences. Yet many still run old in-office Exchange email servers, ageing file servers and VPNs that cause more problems than they solve.

OMNET IT Solutions has completed over 80 cloud migrations for Delhi NCR businesses — from 10-user startups moving to Google Workspace to 200-user companies moving to Microsoft Azure. This guide explains what your Gurugram business should know before you move.

Quick answer: The biggest mistake is moving to the cloud without a plan. A proper move takes 2-6 weeks, depending on how much data you have — not a weekend.

For Gurugram businesses with 20-300 users, Microsoft 365 Business Premium gives the best value. One licence covers email, teamwork, device management (Intune) and security (Defender). Google Workspace suits teams already using Google or mostly Macs, who need desktop Office apps less.

What a Proper Cloud Migration Involves

Phase 1: Discovery and Planning (Week 1)

OMNET engineers list what you have today: in-office servers, shared drives, your email system, current licences and number of users. We also find what depends on them — apps that need a server, office printers, and old software that may not work in the cloud. This avoids 'we did not know about that' surprises halfway through.

Phase 2: Account Setup and Security (Week 1-2)

We set up your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace tenant (your company account). This covers your domain, email routing, MFA (a second login check), access rules, email filtering and spam protection. Locking down security at the start is vital. The default settings are not safe enough for business use.

Phase 3: Data Migration (Week 2-4)

We move email with tools like BitTitan MigrationWiz or built-in Exchange migration. Moving files to SharePoint or Google Drive means sorting data, copying access rights and switching over in stages. For large Gurugram businesses (100+ users), we move one department at a time to limit disruption.

Phase 4: User Training and Cutover (Week 3-5)

Getting staff to use the new system is the most overlooked step. A clean move still fails if people do not know how to use it. OMNET runs training for each department (30-60 minutes per team). After the switch, you get 2 weeks of hypercare (extra priority support).

Phase 5: Retiring Old In-Office Systems (Week 4-6)

We switch off your old in-office systems only after checking all data is moved and everyone works in the cloud. Old systems stay readable for 30 days before shutdown, to catch anything missed.

Common Cloud Migration Mistakes Gurugram Businesses Make

  • Not hardening security at setup: By default, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace allow old login methods that skip MFA. OMNET blocks these from day one.
  • Migrating without a data cleanup: Moving 10 years of email and files without archiving old data raises costs and slows search. Set an email archive policy before you move.
  • Ignoring printers and legacy apps: Network printers and older software often cannot log in with cloud accounts. List these before you switch over.
  • No training plan: Staff left alone with SharePoint or Google Drive start using personal Dropbox or WhatsApp to share files. Training is a must.
  • Cutting ISP redundancy: In the cloud, your internet link matters more, not less. After the move, Gurugram businesses need two internet providers with automatic switch-over.

Frequently Asked Questions

What affects the cost of Microsoft 365 migration for a 30-user Gurugram business?

A full Microsoft 365 Business Premium migration is a one-time project, and licences are billed per user per month on top. The cost depends on the number of users, how much email and file data you have, and the level of security and training you need. The project covers account setup, security, email migration, SharePoint setup, staff training and support after the move. As a Microsoft CSP, OMNET can sometimes offer better licence prices than buying direct from Microsoft. Call us for a free, no-obligation quote.

Can we migrate from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365 (or vice versa)?

Yes. OMNET has moved Gurugram clients both ways between Microsoft 365 and Google. Moving email is simple. Moving files between Drive and SharePoint needs more planning, because file details and access rights must carry over. For a 30-50 user company, allow 4-6 weeks.

Will cloud migration disrupt our Gurugram business operations?

A well-planned move causes very little disruption. Old and new systems run side by side for a while, and we do the final email switch over a weekend. Most 30-50 user Gurugram businesses see less than 1 hour of disruption. Our 2-week hypercare period after the switch catches and fixes any issues fast.
