The 10-Point Cybersecurity Checklist for Noida Businesses

1. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on All Business Accounts

MFA is the single most effective control against account takeover. Enable it on Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, banking portals, cloud services, and your VPN. Over 90% of successful account compromises involve accounts without MFA. Setup time: 30 minutes per user. Cost: included in Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.

2. Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR/XDR) on Every Device

Basic antivirus is dead. Modern ransomware bypasses signature-based AV in minutes. Every Noida business device — laptops, desktops, and servers — needs behavioral EDR such as CrowdStrike Falcon, Sophos Intercept X, or Microsoft Defender for Business. If you are running free antivirus on business machines, you are unprotected.

3. Firewall Configured — Not Just Installed

Many Noida offices have a firewall that was installed but never properly configured. A factory-default firewall with open rules is almost as bad as no firewall. Your firewall should have explicit deny-all outbound rules, application awareness, and IPS signatures enabled. OMNET engineers audit and reconfigure firewalls from Fortinet, Sophos, Cisco, and pfSense.

4. Patch Management — Windows and Software Updated Within 14 Days

The WannaCry ransomware that crippled businesses worldwide exploited a vulnerability that Microsoft had patched 60 days earlier. No patch = open door. Set Windows Update to automatic, and audit third-party software (Chrome, Adobe, Java) monthly. Unpatched software is the second most common entry point after phishing.

5. Encrypted, Tested Data Backups — Off-Site

The key word is tested. Many Noida businesses have backups they have never restored from. A backup you cannot restore from is not a backup. Use the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies of data, 2 different media types, 1 offsite or cloud. Test restoration quarterly. OMNET manages cloud backup for Noida clients from Rs 3,500 per month.

6. Email Filtering with Anti-Phishing Protection

Over 80% of cyberattacks start with a phishing email. Microsoft 365 Defender and Google Workspace include anti-phishing — but they need to be configured beyond defaults. Enable DMARC, DKIM, and SPF records on your domain to prevent your own domain being used for phishing. If you have not set these three DNS records, your email domain can be spoofed.

7. Privileged Access Controls — Not Everyone Is Admin

In many Noida SMB offices, every employee runs as a local administrator. This means any malware they run gets admin privileges instantly. Set up standard user accounts for daily work and a separate admin account for IT tasks only. This single change stops an entire class of malware.

8. VPN for Remote Access — No Open RDP

Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) exposed directly to the internet on port 3389 is one of the most scanned and attacked services on the internet. If your Noida team accesses office systems remotely via RDP without a VPN, you have an open door. Use a VPN gateway (Fortinet, OpenVPN, Cisco AnyConnect) and disable direct RDP exposure.

9. Employee Security Awareness Training — Annually Minimum

Your employees are your largest attack surface. One click on a phishing link can bypass every technical control you have. Run phishing simulation tests and annual security awareness training. OMNET delivers security awareness workshops for Noida businesses starting at Rs 8,000 for groups up to 25.

10. Incident Response Plan — Written and Tested

When (not if) a security incident occurs, the first 30 minutes are critical. A written incident response plan tells your team exactly what to do: who to call, what to isolate, how to preserve evidence, and when to notify clients or regulators. As a CERT-In empanelled provider, OMNET can help you build and test an IR plan that meets Indian regulatory requirements.
