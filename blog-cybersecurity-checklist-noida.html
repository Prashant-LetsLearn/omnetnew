The 10-Point Cybersecurity Checklist for Noida Businesses

1. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on All Business Accounts

MFA (a second check at login, like a code on your phone) is the best way to stop someone taking over an account. Turn it on for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, banking portals, cloud services and your VPN. Over 90% of successful account break-ins hit accounts without MFA. Setup time: 30 minutes per user. Cost: included in Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.

2. Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR/XDR) on Every Device

Basic antivirus is no longer enough. Modern ransomware gets past it in minutes. Every Noida business laptop, desktop and server needs EDR (security software that spots and stops suspicious behaviour), such as CrowdStrike Falcon, Sophos Intercept X or Microsoft Defender for Business. If your business machines run free antivirus, you are unprotected.

3. Firewall Configured — Not Just Installed

Many Noida offices have a firewall that was installed but never set up properly. A firewall on factory settings with open rules is almost as bad as none. Block all outgoing traffic by default and allow only what you need. Turn on app control and IPS (intrusion prevention). OMNET engineers check and reconfigure Fortinet, Sophos, Cisco and pfSense firewalls.

4. Patch Management — Windows and Software Updated Within 14 Days

The WannaCry ransomware hit businesses worldwide through a flaw Microsoft had fixed 60 days earlier. No update = open door. Set Windows Update to automatic. Check other software (Chrome, Adobe, Java) every month. Out-of-date software is the second most common way in, after phishing.

5. Encrypted, Tested Data Backups — Off-Site

The key word is tested. Many Noida businesses have backups they have never restored. If you cannot restore it, it is not a backup. Follow the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies of your data, on 2 different types of storage, with 1 off-site or in the cloud. Test a restore every quarter. OMNET manages cloud backup for Noida clients from Rs 3,500 per month.

6. Email Filtering with Anti-Phishing Protection

Over 80% of cyberattacks start with a phishing email. Microsoft 365 Defender and Google Workspace include anti-phishing, but the default settings are not enough. Add DMARC, DKIM and SPF records to your domain. These stop criminals sending fake emails in your company's name. Without these three DNS records, your email domain can be faked.

7. Privileged Access Controls — Not Everyone Is Admin

In many Noida offices, every employee is a local administrator on their PC. So any malware they open gets full control at once. Give staff standard accounts for daily work. Keep a separate admin account for IT tasks only. This one change stops a whole class of malware.

8. VPN for Remote Access — No Open RDP

RDP (Remote Desktop) left open to the internet on port 3389 is one of the most attacked services online. If your Noida team logs in to office systems over RDP without a VPN, you have an open door. Use a VPN gateway (Fortinet, OpenVPN, Cisco AnyConnect) and close direct RDP access.

9. Employee Security Awareness Training — Annually Minimum

Your staff are your biggest risk. One click on a phishing link can get past every technical control you have. Run fake phishing tests and yearly security awareness training. OMNET runs security awareness workshops for Noida businesses starting at Rs 8,000 for groups up to 25.

10. Incident Response Plan — Written and Tested

When (not if) a security incident happens, the first 30 minutes matter most. A written incident response plan tells your team exactly what to do: who to call, what to disconnect, how to keep evidence, and when to tell clients or regulators. As a CERT-In empanelled provider, OMNET can help you build and test a plan that meets Indian rules.
